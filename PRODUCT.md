# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Foldkit developers and teams who want the familiar shadcn/ui component language without React or JSX. They value explicit Elm-style models, updates, effects, and testable behavior while retaining copy-owned component source and compatibility with shadcn themes.

## Product Purpose

crease/ui rebuilds the shadcn/ui design language on Foldkit UI. It provides accessible, styled Foldkit view functions and explicit stateful components, plus documentation, examples, charts, blocks, registry distribution, and machine-readable guidance. Success means developers can adopt or adapt polished components while understanding and owning both their styling and their state architecture.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, and slightly dry. The product earns confidence through rendered proof, readable source, explicit tradeoffs, and close visual parity rather than hype.

## Anti-references

Avoid cute origami imagery, busy craft-project aesthetics, hidden framework magic, gratuitous decoration, inflated marketing language, and unfamiliar component behavior. Do not obscure the cost or structure of Foldkit's explicit state wiring.

## Design Principles

- Practice what the library promises: examples must be real Foldkit components with complete, readable source.
- Show proof before claims through live components, parity comparisons, tests, and transparent architecture.
- Preserve earned familiarity from shadcn/ui while making Foldkit-specific behavior explicit.
- Keep styling themeable through semantic tokens and keep state observable through the Elm architecture.
- Prefer ownership and inspectability over opaque convenience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 Level AA for flagship and shared surfaces. Preserve keyboard navigation, focus management, semantic structure, ARIA behavior from Foldkit UI primitives, readable contrast, responsive containment, and reduced-motion preferences.
